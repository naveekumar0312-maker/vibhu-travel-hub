About Kerala

Kerala is a beautiful blend of tranquil backwaters, mist-covered hills, tropical beaches and rich cultural traditions, offering travellers an experience that combines relaxation, nature and discovery.

Stretching along the Malabar Coast of the Arabian Sea, Kerala features palm-shaded backwater lagoons, spice-scented mountain peaks, and lush wildlife reserves. From the historic commercial port of Cochin to the royal capital of Trivandrum, Kerala travel creates memories for family vacations, honeymoons, and Ayurvedic retreats.

The crowning glory among Kerala tourist places is its world-renowned backwaters. Cruising on Kettuvallam houseboats in Alleppey and Kumarakom, exploring tea plantations in Munnar, spotting wildlife in Thekkady, or relaxing on red cliff beaches in Varkala and Kovalam showcase the state's natural variety. Cultural capital Thrissur and spice hub Calicut complete the rich cultural mosaic.
