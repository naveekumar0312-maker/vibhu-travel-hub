About Karnataka

Karnataka brings together historic architecture, misty hill stations, coffee plantations, wildlife reserves, coastal landscapes and vibrant modern cities, making it one of South India's most diverse travel destinations.

Situated on the Deccan Plateau, Karnataka is celebrated for its imperial history and natural beauty. From India's cosmopolitan Silicon Valley in Bangalore to the royal palace streets of Mysore, Karnataka travel offers an inspiring journey through magnificent heritage.

The state's imperial legacy shines at illuminated Mysore Palace, Chamundi Hill, and the UNESCO World Heritage stone ruins of Hampi. Nature lovers escape to the coffee plantation hills of Coorg and Chikmagalur, trek Mullayanagiri peak, explore Kabini tiger safaris, or unwind along the coastal cliff beaches of Gokarna and Udupi.
