ABOUT SERVICE

Comfortable Long Drives with Vibhu

Going out of town? Forget the hassle of driving long distances or the rigid schedules of public transport. Vibhu Travel Hub offers dedicated outstation cabs customized entirely for your itinerary.

We provide two distinct services: Round-Trip Tours for vacations where the cab stays with you, and One-Way Drops for when you just need to get to another city securely. Our fleet ranges from comfortable Sedans for solo travelers to spacious SUVs and Tempo Travellers for larger groups.

Our drivers are route-experts across Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and Karnataka, and are fluent in multiple regional languages.
