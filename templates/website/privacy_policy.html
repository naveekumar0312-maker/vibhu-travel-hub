Privacy Policy

Your privacy and trust are important to us.

At Vibhu Travel Hub, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide while using our website and travel services.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us, submit an enquiry, request a quotation, make a booking enquiry or use our travel services, we may collect information such as your name, phone number, email address, travel details, pickup location, destination, travel date and other information you voluntarily provide.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to respond to enquiries, process booking requests, provide quotations, arrange transportation services, communicate with you about your request and improve our website and services.

3. Communication

If you submit your contact details through our website, we may contact you by phone, email or WhatsApp regarding your enquiry, booking request or requested travel service.

4. Information Protection

We take reasonable steps to protect the information submitted through our website from unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services or platforms such as WhatsApp, Google services, analytics tools, maps or other external services. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, understand website usage and improve user experience.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, provide services, maintain business records and meet applicable legal or operational requirements.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us if you have questions about the personal information you have provided or if you would like to request clarification regarding how your information is used.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. We encourage parents or guardians to supervise children's use of online services.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, website functionality or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact Vibhu Travel Hub.
